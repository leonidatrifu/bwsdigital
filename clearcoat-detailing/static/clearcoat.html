Process

How a correction goes

  1. Inspect

    We check every panel under LED light and log paint thickness in microns.

  2. Decontaminate

    Iron remover and clay bar lift what washing leaves behind.

  3. Correct

    Machine polishing removes swirls while staying within safe clear coat limits.

  4. Protect

    Coating goes on panel by panel, then cures overnight before you drive.
